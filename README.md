# Fadel Food Trading – Odoo ERP Purchase & Inventory Management

## Project Overview

A practical ERP training project simulating procurement, inventory control, and warehouse operations for a fictional Saudi food trading company.

The project was implemented using **Odoo 20.0 Enterprise Edition**, focusing on purchasing, inventory management, warehouse operations, and product traceability.

## Business Scenario

Fadel Food Trading is a fictional food distribution company that purchases food products from suppliers, manages warehouse operations, monitors inventory levels, and delivers products to customers.

The project simulates daily business operations using Odoo ERP.

## Tools & Technologies

- Odoo 20.0 Enterprise Edition
- Odoo Purchase Module
- Odoo Inventory Module
- Odoo Sales Module (for delivery workflow simulation)

## Project Scope

### 1. Master Data Management
- Company and warehouse setup.
- Product categories and product creation.
- Supplier management.
- Product units of measure.
- Lot and batch tracking setup.

### 2. Procurement Management
- Request for Quotation (RFQ).
- Supplier quotation comparison.
- Supplier selection.
- Purchase Order creation and confirmation.
- Three-step goods receipt.
- Supplier purchase returns.

### 3. Inventory Management
- Internal stock transfers.
- Physical inventory counting.
- Inventory adjustments.
- Stock by Location reporting.
- Inventory Moves History.
- Reordering Rules configuration and review.

### 4. Warehouse Operations
- Three-step receiving workflow.
- Quality Control operations.
- Warehouse storage.
- Picking operations.
- Packing operations.
- Outbound delivery.

### 5. Product Traceability & Expiration Management
- Lot and batch tracking.
- Expiration date configuration.
- FEFO (First Expired, First Out) strategy.
- Expiration date monitoring.

### 6. Inventory Exceptions
- Scrap management for damaged products.
- Purchase return processing.
- Inventory discrepancy identification and correction.

## Practical Business Scenarios

The project includes practical scenarios involving:

- Purchasing and receiving bulk food products.
- Moving inventory between warehouse locations.
- Delivering customer orders through picking, packing, and shipping.
- Performing physical inventory counts and correcting discrepancies.
- Managing damaged inventory through scrap operations.
- Tracking food product expiration dates and applying FEFO.

This is a self-directed practical training project using a fictional company and simulated business transactions.

It demonstrates hands-on practice with standard Odoo 20.0 Enterprise Edition Purchase and Inventory workflows. It does not represent a real company implementation or professional employment experience.
